## Research

After researching academic articles about student portfolios. I believe my goals are similar to the academic goals and learning outcomes discussed by Belgrad (2013) and Oudkerk Pool (2020). Firstly, Belgrad (2013) explains that portfolios allow students to show their effort, progress, and achievements while reflecting on what they have learned and setting goals for future improvement. This relates to my professional and entrepreneurial goals because I have the mindset to continuously build on my experiences and work toward goals that are closely connected to what I am already doing. My portfolio demonstrates this effort and progress. For example, while working at TD in a customer-facing role, I used what I observed and learned from helping customers to create my own TD UX case study for my portfolio. I identified challenges customers experienced with digital banking and explored design solutions that could make these services easier and more accessible. This project shows that I am taking steps towards becoming a UX designer at a financial institution, specifically Toronto-Dominion Bank. Secondly, Oudkerk Pool (2020) explains that portfolios do not always show every part of a student's development. This relates to my goals because some experiences can help me grow in ways that I cannot physically see or measure. For example, travelling gives me new experiences, ideas, and inspiration that can influence my designs. Running my own business has also helped me improve my communication, problem-solving, and decision-making skills. These experiences can give me ideas for future UX projects, case studies, design decisions, and examples to talk about during interviews. I also want my UX portfolio to show my personality and what makes me different from other designers. Having experiences in travelling, business, and customer service gives me more to share on my “About Me” page and helps show who I am as a UX designer. Even if I cannot directly show every experience in my portfolio, they still help shape my ideas, skills, and personality. Overall, these two academic articles about student portfolios make me believe my goals are similar in comparison because they can show my progress, experiences, and the steps I am taking toward my future goals as a UX designer.

Citations:
  Belgrad, S. (2013). Portfolios and E-Portfolios: Student Reflection. Self-Assessment, and Goal Setting in the Learning Process. In SAGE Handbook of Research on Classroom Assessment (pp. 331–346).

  Oudkerk Pool, A., Jaarsma, A. D. C., Driessen, E. W., & Govaerts, M. J. B. (2020). Student perspectives on competency-based portfolios: Does a portfolio reflect their competence development? Perspectives on Medical Education, 9(3), 166–172. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-020-00571-7
