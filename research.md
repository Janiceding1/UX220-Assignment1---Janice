## Research

After researching academic articles about student portfolios. I believe my goals are similar to the academic goals and learning outcomes discussed by Belgrad (2013) and Oudkerk Pool (2020). Belgrad (2013) explains that portfolios allow students to show their effort, progress, and achievements while reflecting on what they have learned and setting goals for future improvement. This relates to my professional and entrepreneurial goals because I want to build on experiences I started during university, such as working at TD in a front-facing role and maintaining my 3D printing business. 

Oudkerk Pool (2020) also found that portfolios do not always show every part of a student's development. Students reported that experiences such as informal feedback, conversations with peers, mistakes, difficult situations, and personal qualities such as confidence were often not documented, even though they contributed to their development. This relates to my goals because I also value experiences that are difficult to measure, such as becoming more confident through running a business, learning from customers and coworkers, travelling, and maintaining a healthy work-life balance. The main difference is that academic learning outcomes focus more on demonstrating learning, skills, and competencies, while my goals also focus on my personal life, including travelling and continuing to play racquet sports. Overall, both my goals and the academic learning outcomes focus on continued growth, but I define my development through both professional accomplishments and personal experiences.

Citations:

  Belgrad, S. (2013). Portfolios and E-Portfolios: Student Reflection. Self-Assessment, and Goal Setting in the Learning Process. In SAGE Handbook of Research on Classroom Assessment (pp. 331–346).

  Oudkerk Pool, A., Jaarsma, A. D. C., Driessen, E. W., & Govaerts, M. J. B. (2020). Student perspectives on competency-based portfolios: Does a portfolio reflect their competence development? Perspectives on Medical Education, 9(3), 166–172. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-020-00571-7
