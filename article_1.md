## Professional/Career

![my career...picture by me](TD.png)

Five years after graduating, I want to be able to tell my classmates that I have achieved my career goal of working as a UX designer for a financial institution, specifically Toronto-Dominion Bank. I have continued growing my career at TD after starting in a customer experience role while I was in university. After graduating, I applied internally and was able to move into a full-time UX design role through my experience and connections within the company. Working directly with customers has helped me understand their needs and solve design problems that make digital banking easier and more accessible. I now work full-time in UX and have helped improve TD's website, EasyWeb, and mobile banking experiences. Since moving into UX, I have worked on TD's digital platforms, including the website, EasyWeb, and mobile banking app. I have helped solve design problems to make information easier to find and banking more accessible for people of different ages, backgrounds, and levels of digital experience. Such as ways to reduce language barriers, simplify navigation, and create clear interfaces that help customers feel comfortable and safe when managing their finances online.
